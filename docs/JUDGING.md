# Local judging demonstration

These are the four local award categories supplied by the team. They are not
claims that the project has won an award. Team LOGICA submits manually.

| Category | Delivered work | Suggested demonstration | Limit to state clearly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Global Connection | Public access without accounts; Learn in English, Spanish and Hindi; three guided activities; random activity; shareable observation links and PNG cards. | Open a Learn activity, change language, explore a random activity, and share an observation. | Translation and delivery checks are not evidence of international adoption or native-speaker review. |
| Best Use of Science | Real SPHEREx cutouts, aligned epoch stacks, blink/difference views, Gaia DR3 context, spectra/light curves, uncertainty/quality flags, provenance and independent astrometry checks. | Show Barnard's motion against Gaia, then explain the uncertainty and the provenance of the frames. | Pipeline 3 passes the stated Barnard and epsilon Indi B astrometry gates. WISE detection and RW Aur/R Dra variability are not validated; no Planet X discovery is claimed. |
| Best Use of Technology | Azure Container Apps and Container Registry; Blob-backed prepared images; managed identities; main-only OIDC deployment; monitoring, cost alerts and deployed daily watcher. | Show a prepared target loading with API requests blocked, then the Azure architecture and a successful deployment run. | An uncached Live build still depends on upstream archives. Watcher deployment is verified; successful metadata refresh remains blocked by upstream IRSA failures in the recorded checks. |
| Art & Technology | Interactive sky/orrery presentation, infrared flipbook, blink/difference visualization, wavelength colours and downloadable observation cards. | Follow one visual story from a sky target to changes across epochs and a shared PNG. | Artistic presentation supports inspection; colours and visual motion alone do not establish a scientific detection. |

The current evidence and human-owned submission gates are in
[SUBMISSION.md](SUBMISSION.md). Browser delivery checks cover all 47 prepared
catalog fields, the five default presets and nine language/activity combinations.
They do not extend scientific validation to every catalog field. Record the
presentation/video and confirm the final form on the hackathon site separately.
